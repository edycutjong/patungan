A group payment where the last seat pays the seller in the same transaction, and a missed deadline refunds everyone.

Live on QIE mainnet · contract 0x2f4646D52a5393E239f5b880411dC2CA2D945F31 (chain 1990, source verified on Blockscout). No wallet needed anywhere on this path: every page is a read-only call.

The 30-second path

  1. Open the PAID pool PAID IN FULL2 of 2 seats at 0.25 QUSDC. The last seat paid in QIE: one transaction swapped QIE to QUSDC on QIEDEX, took the seat and paid 0.50 QUSDC to qkulk75527.qie: 0x4bd5c37d…a7a0.
  2. Open the REFUNDED pool REFUNDED3 of 6 seats paid, the deadline passed, and one refundAll returned 0.75 QUSDC to all 3 members: 0x7886eba2…0f0a.
  3. Check the verified source on the explorerOr open /verify: it reads solvency, the pool count and the four QIE components (QUSDC, QIEDEX router, Zone .qie names, QIE Oracle) live from the contract.
  4. Start your own pool at /newOnly this step asks for a wallet (QIE Wallet preferred, any EIP-1193 wallet works).

Honest limits

  • Both mainnet pools are the builder's demo pools: every member wallet is a builder wallet (labelled in the app), not a user, and the PAID pool paid the builder's own name qkulk75527.qie, not a real seller.
  • No real group has used it on mainnet yet. Gas and price-guard numbers other than the ones marked mainnet were measured on a fork.
  • QUSDC has an issuer-controlled VAULT_ROLE. If the issuer burns escrowed QUSDC, a pool cannot make everyone whole; Patungan then refuses new joins (isSolvent()) and has no admin key of its own.
