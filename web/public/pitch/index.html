Patungan is the Indonesian word for chipping in. One link per group: friends pay seats, and the pool either pays the venue in the last seat's transaction or gives every friend their money back.

Masalahnya · the problem

Group money lives in a chat

  • The organizer fronts the moneyon trust
  • Someone chases every friend"belum transfer" · not paid yet
  • If the plan dies, refunds go by handone transfer at a time
Patungan
Every futsal group, arisan or office collection has one person who pays first and then collects. Nothing makes the friends pay, and nothing guarantees the refunds if the booking falls through.

The swap is invisible to the member. The Oracle is a ceiling, never the price paid: if the pool's quote is too far above fair value, the join reverts instead of overpaying.
