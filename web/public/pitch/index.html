Patungan is the Indonesian word for chipping in. One link per group: friends pay seats, and the pool either pays the venue in the last seat's transaction or gives every friend their money back. The card is pool #1 on QIE mainnet, run from builder wallets.

The group chat, today

Group money lives in a chat

  • The organizer fronts the moneyon trust
  • Someone chases every friend"have you paid yet?", one at a time
  • If the plan dies, refunds go by handone transfer per friend
Patungan
Picture a Thursday football group, an arisan or an office collection where one person pays first and then collects. Nothing makes the friends pay, and nothing guarantees the refunds if the booking falls through.

This is the real call trace of pool #1's last seat on mainnet. The member attached QIE. The contract checked its own balance, read the QIE Oracle, took a QIEDEX quote 584 basis points over fair value, inside the 1,500 guard, swapped for exactly 0.25 QUSDC and checked it arrived, counted the last seat, paid the whole 0.50 QUSDC to the payee and sent the unspent QIE back. One transaction; any failed check reverts all of it.

All four are live QIE mainnet contracts. QUSDC, QIEDEX and the Oracle were called inside slide 4's transaction; Zone resolved the payee name when pool #1 was created. The QIEDEX router's source is unverified on the explorer, so the Oracle bound matters.
